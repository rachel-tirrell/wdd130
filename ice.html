Rachel Tirrell's ICE

President Oaks Card

For this ICE, Rachel created an informational card about the current prophet and President of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints, President Oaks.
This card provides information about President Oaks, including his birth date, education, and career. There is an image of President Oaks featured on the card as well.

Favorite Devotional Form

For this ICE, Rachel created a form to collect information about her favorite devotional. The form includes fields for the devotional title, author, and a brief description. Users can submit the form to add their favorite devotional to the list.

Halloween Page

Because it is the month of October, Rachel created a Halloween page. The page features Halloween-themed content and activities, full of spooky images and fun Halloween facts.

Country Flags

Rachel used CSS grid to create a page displaying country flags. Before she began, there were simply boxes of color all over the page, and she had to add grids and adjust the columns and rows to create flags from different countries around the world.

Image of Christ Gallery

This link will take you to a page full of images of Jesus Christ. When working on this ICE, Rachel learned how to use Adobe Photoshop to change the size of the image. Having a large file size is visually appealing, but is unecessary for thumbnail images. Therefore, Rachel resized the images to make them smaller and easier to view. Once you click on the thumbnail image, then the full-size image will appear. The full image is the large file, allowing it to be shown full screen without becoming pixelated.

Daily Devotional Sign Up

For this ICE, Rachel created a form to collect information for daily devotional sign up. The form includes fields for the user's name, email address, and a brief message. Users can submit the form to sign up for daily devotionals.
